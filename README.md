🌱 Krushi Sahayak
AI-Powered Crop Disease Detection and Farming Advisory
Krushi Sahayak is an offline-first mobile application designed to help farmers identify crop diseases and pest infestations using images of leaves and other affected plant parts. It provides easy-to-understand information about plant health, possible causes, prevention methods, treatment options, and pesticide recommendations.
The app aims to make AI-powered agricultural guidance accessible to farmers, including those in rural areas with limited or unreliable internet connectivity.
🎯 Problem Statement
Farmers often struggle to identify crop diseases and pests at an early stage. Limited access to agricultural experts, unreliable internet connectivity, and a lack of trustworthy information can lead to incorrect treatments, crop losses, and unnecessary pesticide use.
Krushi Sahayak aims to address these challenges through an accessible, multilingual, AI-powered farming assistant.
💡 Key Features

📸 AI-Based Crop Diagnosis: Analyze plant images to identify the crop and detect possible diseases, pests, or other plant health problems.
📴 Offline-First Access: Designed to provide supported diagnostic and advisory features without requiring a continuous internet connection.
🌐 Online AI Assistance: Use online AI services when connectivity is available to provide additional explanations and assistance.
💊 Pesticide Recommendations: Provide relevant pesticide guidance when chemical treatment is appropriate, prioritizing verified information and safe usage.
🌿 Treatment and Prevention: Explain possible causes, preventive measures, and treatment options in simple language.
🗣️ Multilingual Assistance: Designed to make agricultural guidance accessible in regional languages, including Gujarati and Hindi.
💬 AI Farming Assistant: Help farmers ask follow-up questions and understand recommendations.
📋 Simple User Interface: Keep essential actions accessible through an easy-to-use interface suitable for farmers with varying levels of digital literacy.

⚙️ How It Works

Capture or Upload: The farmer takes a photo or uploads an image of an affected plant.
Identify the Plant: The app attempts to identify the crop and checks whether the image is suitable for diagnosis.
Analyze the Problem: The AI model analyzes the image for supported diseases, pests, or other plant health issues.
Review the Results: The app presents the predicted issue and its confidence level.
Get Agricultural Guidance: The farmer receives relevant prevention measures, treatment options, and pesticide guidance when appropriate.
Ask Follow-Up Questions: The farmer can use the assistant to understand the diagnosis and recommendations.

When the model cannot identify a problem reliably, the app should communicate uncertainty instead of presenting an unsupported diagnosis.
